# Product

## Register

product

## Users

CLARVEX Team is used by store managers, assistant managers, and retail sales associates during daily store operation. Managers need a quick view of store health, critical handovers, team ranking, plans, and communication status. Associates need their daily target, current sales, schedule, checklist, KPIs, and unread announcements from a phone.

## Product Purpose

The product gives a retail team one mobile-first operating surface for KPIs, weekly schedules, daily plans, pending tasks, store log entries, announcements, and associate ranking. Success means a manager can understand the store's day in minutes, and an associate can know what to do next without asking in chat.

## Brand Personality

Premium, clear, and energetic. The app should feel like a modern installed mobile app for retail operations: polished enough for leadership, fast enough for the sales floor, and calm enough to use repeatedly during the day.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic admin panels, Bootstrap-style dashboards, crowded desktop-first layouts, harsh neon-only palettes, noisy decoration, and screens that look like disconnected templates. Do not use real external retail brand data or brand names.

## Design Principles

1. Mobile operation first: every screen should work naturally from a phone with large touch targets and bottom navigation.
2. Show the next action: dashboards should surface targets, critical tasks, unread items, and assigned work without extra digging.
3. Premium through restraint: use glass, blur, soft shadows, pastel accents, and motion to support hierarchy, not to decorate every pixel.
4. Keep roles obvious: managers and associates should see different levels of control, with the same visual system.
5. Build for growth: keep components reusable and data flows ready for PostgreSQL, Prisma, multi-store support, analytics, and notifications.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Aim for WCAG AA contrast, readable body text, visible focus states, reduced-motion friendliness, and touch targets suitable for Android devices. Color should not be the only signal for priority, status, or completion.
